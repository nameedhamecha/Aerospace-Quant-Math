# Aerospace-Quant-Math
### Bridging Celestial Mechanics and Financial Mathematics

**Author:** Namee Dhamecha (Aerospace Engineering, Amity Mumbai)

**The Philosophy:**
Markets are multi-body dynamical systems. Stocks exhibit inertia, volatility is turbulence, and mean-reversion is a restoring force. My background in modeling the stability of Lagrange points (L4/L5) within the Circular Restricted Three-Body Problem (CR3BP) has given me a rigorous framework for analyzing unstable equilibriums. This repository applies those mathematical principles to Quantitative Trading.

**Current Research Focus:**
- Asymptotic Stability of Triangular Lagrange Points (Ongoing Academic Paper)
- Translating Coriolis forces to Market Momentum Indicators
- Monte Carlo simulations for option pricing under stochastic volatility
